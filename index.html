CLP Holdings — FY2023–FY2025 Financial Trend Analysis

A deliberately narrow window: three years, four metrics, one consistent basis for each. CLP's own "Total Earnings" and "Operating Earnings" told almost opposite stories in FY2023, because of a one-off non-cash impairment, not because the business changed. This dashboard is the "what"; the written analysis is the "so what."

Three-year trends

FY2023–FY2025, on the same disclosed basis for each metric across all three years.

Revenue

HK$bn · 2023–2025

Revenue rose 4.4% in 2024 on higher Australian wholesale power prices, then eased back 3.2% in 2025 as those prices normalized. This is the simplest of the three trends here, no accounting complications, just the energy price cycle.

Total Earnings vs. Operating Earnings

HK$bn · solid = statutory profit, dashed = underlying/core business

Total Earnings (statutory) Operating Earnings (underlying)

The gap in 2023 is the whole story: a HK$5.9bn non-cash goodwill impairment on EnergyAustralia dragged Total Earnings far below Operating Earnings that year. Toggle to "Operating Earnings" above: it barely moved across all three years, while Total Earnings swung wildly around it.

Dividend per share

HK$ · 2023–2025

Raised in both years shown here, including 2024, when the FY2023 impairment was still the freshest number on the statutory profit line. A steady payout policy that didn't wait for headline profit to "recover."
