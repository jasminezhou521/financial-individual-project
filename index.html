Six-year trends

FY2020–FY2025, on the most comparable disclosed basis for each metric.

Revenue

HK$bn · 2020–2025

2022's revenue spike is misleading on its own — it came from high Australian wholesale power prices that CLP had to buy at, not sell at profitably. See the earnings chart for why revenue and profit decoupled that year.

Total Earnings vs. Operating Earnings

HK$bn · solid = statutory profit, dashed = underlying/core business

Total Earnings (statutory) Operating Earnings (underlying)

2022's near-wipeout in Total Earnings was a hedging/outage shock in EnergyAustralia, not a core-business collapse — Operating Earnings, which strips out the fair-value swing, tells a much steadier story.

Dividend per share

HK$ · 2020–2025

Raised every single year in this window, including 2022 when statutory profit fell over 90% — classic regulated-utility payout policy, smoothing one-off shocks for shareholders.
